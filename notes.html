1812: Napoleon's Fateful March — Implementation Notes

Implementation Notes

Sequence of Play changes for asynchronous play:

  • The optional rule to place orders simultaneously (page 10) is enforced by default.
  • Since losses are assigned simultaneously, elimination is only triggered if the enemy would have any fresh SPs after determining losses, not the current board state.

Minor rules modifications:

  • Poor Communications (FR #22) now designates an area where France removes a random order, rather than selected a placed French order.
